About

My work in Canada includes public health research, programme evaluation, population health surveillance, policy analysis and knowledge translation. At Haldimand-Norfolk Health Unit, I prepared epidemiological summaries, maintained public-facing surveillance reports and supported accountability reporting. At Peel Public Health, I developed approaches to help Family Health teams use population health data in programme planning and service delivery. This work drew on the Ontario Public Health Standards, with attention to population health assessment, health equity and evidence-informed decision-making.

I also bring experience in health programme design, implementation and evaluation in Ghana and the Central African Republic. Since 2013, I have conducted qualitative and quantitative research, developed monitoring and evaluation frameworks, worked with government and community partners, and supported health promotion and social marketing programmes. My work involves developing research questions, selecting methods, collecting and analyzing data, and using findings to inform programmes, policy and practice.

I hold a PhD in Population Health from the University of Ottawa, a Master of Public Health and a Bachelor of Science in Administration from the University of Ghana. I also teach health research methods at the University of Ottawa.
